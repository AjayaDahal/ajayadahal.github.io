Key Design Decisions
🖨️

3D-Printed Airframe

PLA-printed fuselage and wing sections assembled with CA glue and reinforced at stress points. Allows rapid prototyping of airframe modifications and repairs without waiting for foam or balsa kits.

⚖️

CG & Wing Loading

Center of gravity positioned at 25-30% Mean Aerodynamic Chord for stable flight. Wing loading calculated to ensure adequate lift at target airspeed while keeping stall speed manageable for park flying.

⚡

Power System Selection

Motor KV, propeller pitch/diameter, ESC rating, and battery C-rating matched for target thrust-to-weight ratio. Aim for 1.2-1.5:1 for sport flying with adequate climb performance.

📻

RC Radio Integration

Aileron, elevator, rudder, and throttle channels with servo throws tuned by expo and dual-rate settings. Failsafe configured to cut throttle and set slight up-elevator on signal loss.

Video Player Pro for Learners

An Android video player for working through a folder of lecture videos. It remembers which videos you have already watched, and has YouTube search built in so a quick lookup does not pull you out of the app.

  • Android
  • ExoPlayer2
  • YouTube API
  • Android Studio
Original write-up on Hackster

Why I built it

It started when a professor sent me 15 lecture videos, on top of other tutorial series I was watching, and I could not keep track of which ones I had finished. Nobody watches 15 videos in one day; a little every day works better, but only if you can see where you left off. When the bookkeeping gets in the way, the motivation to keep learning fades.

I had free time in summer 2020, after my co-op at Hunter Engineering, so I built a player that tracks it for you.

What it does

Playback
Plays the videos on your phone with Google ExoPlayer2
Progress
Marks which videos you have already watched
Search
YouTube search inside the app, so looking something up mid-lecture does not mean switching apps
Release
Published on Google Play in 2020; no longer listed

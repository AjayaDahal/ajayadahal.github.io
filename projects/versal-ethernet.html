Versal Ethernet Reference Designs

AMD's public Ethernet example designs for Versal evaluation boards, which I maintained as a Sr. Embedded Product Application Engineer: from 1G PS GEM designs up to 4×25G and 1×100G on the MRMAC and DCMAC hard IP, rebuilt for each Vivado release with build scripts, validation results and known issues.
